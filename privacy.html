Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-10. Anlatan Helper ("the bot", "we") is a moderation and community bot operated by Anlatan within our Discord server. This policy explains, to you as a member of that server, what data the bot accesses and how it is handled.

1. Scope

This policy covers Anlatan Helper as it operates in the Anlatan Discord server. It applies to members of that server. The bot is private and is not offered for use in other servers.

2. What we access

  • When you join. Your user ID, username and account creation date, which the bot checks against the server's join rules (minimum account age and staff-maintained allow and block lists). Receiving join events requires Discord's Server Members intent.
  • When you change your name. Your old and new username, display name or server nickname, so staff can spot impersonation and recognise members who leave and rejoin under a different name.
  • The member list. Kept in memory so staff tools can match usernames, for example when checking a list of users or adding a role to many members at once.
  • Message text, in one place only. In channels where staff have restricted link previews, the bot reads messages to find links. Reading message text requires Discord's Message Content intent.
  • Contest reactions. The bot adds an upvote to contest entries and counts upvotes when staff tally results.

The bot does not use the Presence intent and does not access your online status, activity, voice activity, or anything from outside Discord. It does not read direct messages you send it. It may send you a direct message: a welcome message when you join, or a short explanation before it removes your account, depending on the server's settings.

4. Storage and security

  • The records listed above with a retention period are kept in a database on private infrastructure that only the bot's operators can access. A daily job deletes them when their period ends.
  • Message text is never written to the bot's database. The only copies of member messages the bot makes are moderation records inside the server's own moderator-only channels.
  • Credentials (the bot token) are stored as host environment configuration, never in source code, and are not shared with third parties.
  • The bot does not sell, rent, or share data with any third party, and no analytics or advertising services are used. Data the bot accesses is never used to train machine learning or AI models.

5. Children's privacy

The bot is intended for use in communities that comply with Discord's minimum age requirements and is not directed at children under those requirements.

6. Your choices

Leaving the server stops any new data being collected about you. If you were removed and believe it was in error, or you have questions or requests about data the bot has handled relating to you (including deletion of stored records or moderation records), message our Modmail bot in the Anlatan Discord server.
