Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-10. Anlatan Helper ("the bot", "we") is a moderation bot operated by Anlatan in the Anlatan Discord server. This policy explains what information the bot handles and why.

1. What we collect

  • Account information: your Discord user ID, username, display name, server nickname and account creation date.
  • Name changes: changes to your username, display name or nickname while you are a member of the server.
  • Messages, in limited cases: in some channels, the bot checks messages for links in order to manage link previews.

The bot does not collect your online status, activity or voice activity, and it does not read direct messages you send it.

2. How we use it

We use this information to keep the server safe and moderated: screening new members, preventing impersonation and ban evasion, and helping moderators manage the server. The bot may send you a direct message, for example a welcome message or the reason for a moderation action.

3. How long we keep it

  • Records that identify you, such as name changes and moderation actions, are deleted from our systems after 45 days.
  • Your current username and display name are kept while you are a member, and deleted 45 days after you leave.
  • Moderation list entries added by staff are kept until staff remove them.
  • Moderation logs, which can include the message that led to an action, are posted in the server's staff-only channels and stay there until staff delete them.
  • Anonymous statistics that cannot identify you may be kept longer.

Message content is never stored in our systems.

4. Sharing

Your information is seen only by the server's staff, and is processed by Discord as part of running its platform. We do not sell or share it with anyone else, and we never use it for advertising or to train machine learning or AI models.

5. Your choices

Leaving the server stops any new information being collected about you. To ask about the information we hold, or to have it deleted, message our Modmail bot in the Anlatan Discord server. If you can no longer reach the server, email [FALLBACK EMAIL]. Moderation list entries may be kept where needed to protect the server, as permitted by law.

6. Children's privacy

The bot is intended for communities that follow Discord's minimum age requirements and is not directed at children below them.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected here with a new effective date.

8. Contact

Anlatan — Modmail in the Anlatan Discord server, or [FALLBACK EMAIL] if you can't reach it.
