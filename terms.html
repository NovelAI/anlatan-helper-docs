2. What the bot does

  • Join screening. When you join, the bot checks your account against the server's rules (minimum account age and staff-maintained allow and block lists) and may remove accounts that don't meet them. It may send you a direct message explaining why, or a welcome message if you stay.
  • Name-change logging. Username, display-name and nickname changes are posted to a moderator-only channel to help staff spot impersonation and ban evasion.
  • Staff tools. Staff can check whether users are in, or banned from, the server, and add or remove a role for many members at once.
  • Link-preview control. In channels staff choose, the bot removes link previews unless the link's site is on the server's allowlist. Your message itself is left in place.
  • Community contests. The bot adds an upvote to contest entries and tallies the votes.

3. Acceptable use

  • Do not attempt to evade moderation, for example by rejoining under a new name or a different account after being removed, or by impersonating other members or staff.
  • Do not attempt to overload, exploit, reverse-engineer, or otherwise interfere with the bot or its moderation.
  • Do not attempt to manipulate contest votes.
  • Follow the server's rules and the Discord Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

4. Automated moderation, human oversight, and appeals

Join screening is automated: the bot can remove an account from the server based on its age and on the server's allow and block lists, without a moderator reviewing each case first. A removal is a kick, not a ban. Every decision is logged to a moderator-only channel for human review.
