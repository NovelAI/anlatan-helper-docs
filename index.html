Anlatan Helper

Moderation and community tools for the Anlatan Discord server.

What it does

  • Join screening: checks new members against the server's account-age rule and staff-maintained allow and block lists, and removes accounts that don't meet them.
  • Name-change log: posts username, display-name and nickname changes to a moderator-only channel, to help staff spot impersonation and ban evasion.
  • Staff tools: check whether a list of users is in, or banned from, the server, and add or remove a role for many members at once.
  • Link-preview control: removes link previews in chosen channels unless the site is allowlisted.
  • Community contests: upvotes entries, tallies results, and draws themes from the suggestions thread.

Privacy at a glance

  • Uses the Server Members intent for join screening, the name-change log and staff tools, and the Message Content intent only to find links in link-restricted channels and to read the theme-suggestions thread. It does not use the Presence intent.
  • Message text is processed in memory and never stored by the bot.
  • Name history and removal records are kept for 45 days, then deleted; the long-term record is moderation logs in the server's own moderator-only channels.
  • No data is sold or shared, and none is used to train machine learning or AI models.
