Anlatan Helper

Moderation for the Anlatan Discord server.

What it does

Anlatan Helper is a private moderation bot for the Anlatan Discord server. It helps our moderators screen new members, stay informed of name changes, and manage link previews.

Privacy at a glance

  • Records of name changes and moderation actions are deleted from our systems after 45 days.
  • Message content is never stored in our systems.
  • Nothing is sold or shared, and nothing is used to train AI models.
